# Align the sidebar controls

- The sidebar toggle, category and module arrows, and task counts share a column. Download and category rows place their trailing controls closer to the edge, matching the inset of their leading icons.
- Windows aligns New download with the task search field and uses equal margins between sidebar rows and the adjacent panel. Group headings and counts are easier to scan. In narrow windows, queue actions sit together on the second toolbar row.

## Verification

- Windows workspace tests compare the visible panel margins and the alignment of New download and Search in both densities.

- The file-group browser test measures the expanded control centers and side insets with and without a scrollbar, and checks collapsed icon alignment across five viewport widths.
